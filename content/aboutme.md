# About Me


I’m Piotr, a software engineer in Berlin.

At a young age, I saw a computer for the first time on a TV news program in Poland, where I come from. Later, at a hospital, I witnessed an abdominal ultrasound being performed. Both experiences left me fascinated by these mysterious machines. I couldn’t get them out of my head.

Owning a computer seemed like a distant dream. Luckily, my loving parents noticed my interest, and before long, I had my first computer: the [Timex Computer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timex_Computer_2048), with a monochrome display, a cassette recorder, some games and a user manual.

I devoured the manual. It was both an exciting and a frustrating experience. One day, I accidentally pressed a key combination, and a full screen of text in English appeared: a list of BASIC instructions. I had no idea what I was looking at, but the experience gave me a strong motivation to learn English. I wanted to understand the machine! English wasn’t taught in schools at the time, but my loving parents took note again and signed me up for English classes outside of school.

And so it began. I learned BASIC from the Timex manual. Later came the PC, Turbo Pascal, and endless hours playing games.

Today, I’m a software engineer. Over my career, I’ve built desktop applications in Java, programmed payment terminals in C, maintained a complex CMake-based build system, packaged software, developed internal tools in Python, and created and maintained CI/CD pipelines with Jenkins. I’ve also helped less technically versed colleagues.

As LLMs increasingly change how software is made, I’m figuring out where my experience and curiosity fit.

In my free time, I enjoy spending time with my children, flying FPV and practicing judo.

# About This Site

I started this site to document my learning journey, practice writing, and as a way to keep notes for myself. I enjoy exploring a wide range of topics. Consider this a digital garden. A hodgepodge, driven by curiosity.
